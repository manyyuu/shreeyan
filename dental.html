DENTAL MYTHS & FACTS

Common beliefs.
Clearer facts.

A few quick oral-health truths that can help you decide what to ask your dentist.

MYTH / FACT01

“Bleeding gums are normal.”

FACT Bleeding can be a sign of gum disease. If it keeps happening, arrange a dental check rather than ignoring it.

NHS: Gum disease ↗
MYTH / FACT02

“The harder I brush, the cleaner my teeth.”

FACT Gentle brushing with fluoride toothpaste is the goal. Brushing too hard may damage enamel and irritate gums.

NHS dental quiz ↗
MYTH / FACT03

“No toothache means no tooth decay.”

FACT Early tooth decay may cause no symptoms. Regular check-ups can help a dentist spot problems before they become painful.

NHS: Tooth decay ↗
