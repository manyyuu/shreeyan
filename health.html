THE HEALTH NOTES

Small things.
Worth knowing.

Short, clear reminders to help you prepare for better conversations about your health.

MYTH / FACT01

“I should wait until I feel really unwell.”

A better starting point: If a symptom or change concerns you, ask a qualified clinician when it should be assessed.

EVERYDAY HABITS02

Make your routine easier to repeat.

Follow the plan agreed with your clinician, keep track of recommended readings, and choose manageable routines you can sustain.

BEFORE A VISIT03

Your questions belong in the room.

Write down what changed, when it started, the medicines you take and the questions you do not want to forget.

READY FOR DIALOGUE

Feel more prepared
for your visit.

A consultation is a conversation. You do not have to arrive with all the answers — start with what you have noticed and what you want to understand.

01 / BEFORE
↗

Prepare what you need.

Bring recent reports if you have them, a current medicine list, relevant readings and your questions.

02 / DURING
↗

Know what to expect.

Share your concern, discuss your history and ask the doctor to explain anything that is unclear.

03 / AFTER
↗

Understand what comes next.

Before leaving, clarify the agreed plan, any recommended monitoring, when to follow up and what changes should prompt medical advice.
